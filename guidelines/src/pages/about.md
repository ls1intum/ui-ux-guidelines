# About Us

## Applied Education Technologies (AET)

These guidelines are developed and maintained by the UI-UX Cross Project Team of the [Applied Education Technologies (AET)](https://aet.cit.tum.de/) research group at the TUM School of Computation, Information and Technology.
They apply to all web applications developed at AET, for example the projects in the [ls1intum GitHub organization](https://github.com/ls1intum).

Postal address: Boltzmannstrasse 3, 85748 Garching, Germany

## Contribute

The guidelines are open source. To suggest a change, report a problem, or add a new page, open an issue or a pull request in the [ui-ux-guidelines repository](https://github.com/ls1intum/ui-ux-guidelines). Every guideline page also has an *Edit this page* link at the bottom.
